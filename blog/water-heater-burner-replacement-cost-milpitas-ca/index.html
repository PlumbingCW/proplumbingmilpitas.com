Water Heater Burner Replacement Cost: Milpitas, CA Price Guide

A gas water heater burner is the assembly that lights and heats the bottom of the tank. When it fails, the tank is cold and you wonder whether to fix it or replace the heater. The answer depends on the age of the tank, the part that failed and what the repair costs against a new unit. This guide shows how to think about it, without guessing at a price.

What the burner assembly includes

On a gas tank heater, the burner assembly sits behind a small access door at the bottom. It includes the burner itself, the pilot or igniter, the thermocouple that senses the flame and the gas control valve that opens and closes the gas. Many "burner problems" turn out to be a dirty pilot, a failed thermocouple or a faulty control valve, not the burner itself.

Each part has a different cost, so the first step is a diagnosis. Cleaning the burner, replacing a thermocouple and replacing the gas control valve are three different jobs with three different price levels. A technician should tell you which part failed before quoting.

Common failures and what they usually involve

SymptomLikely partTypical remedy
Pilot will not stay litThermocouple or dirty pilotClean or replace the thermocouple.
Burner will not igniteIgniter, gas valve or no gasTest and replace the failed part.
Yellow, lazy flameDirty burner or poor air supplyClean the burner and check combustion air.
Burner runs but water stays coolSediment layer or failed thermostatFlush the tank and test the control.
Rumble or pop noisesSediment on the tank bottomFlush or consider replacement.
Soot on the tank bottomPoor combustionStop using it and have it inspected.

Soot, a strong gas smell or carbon monoxide alarms are safety problems. Turn off the gas, ventilate and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

Repair or replace: the age rule

A common rule of thumb says that if the heater is over 10 years old and the repair is more than half the price of a new heater, replace it. The tank is the part that you cannot repair, and an old tank can fail soon after a repair. A heater under 8 years old with a failed burner part is usually worth fixing.

Consider what else you will do. A replacement is the time to move to a more efficient model, to add a drain pan and an expansion tank, and to bring the install up to current code. See water heater replacement and water heater repair to compare the two paths.

Local note

A Milpitas rule that affects the decision

The Bay Area Air Quality Management District has adopted zero-NOx limits for new gas water heaters. The date for new gas tank water heaters has been delayed to 2028, according to the district. That delay means a gas tank is still available to buy in Milpitas today, but the future points to electric and heat pump models.

A burner repair on a gas heater that is still sound extends its life without forcing that choice.

The City of Milpitas also requires a plumbing permit to replace a water heater, with seismic strapping, a relief valve discharge and a seismic gas shutoff valve where required. A burner repair on an existing heater is a repair, not a replacement, so the permit is typically not needed.

Check with the city if the repair involves moving or changing the vent. For homes in North San Jose, see our San Jose service page.

Safety signs that mean stop and call

  • A smell of gas near the heaterLeave the house and call the gas utility or 911.
  • Soot or scorch marks around the burner doorCombustion is not clean.
  • A carbon monoxide alarm soundsGet everyone outside and call for help.
  • Orange or yellow flame with a lazy shapeThe burner needs cleaning or the air supply is blocked.
  • Water pooling at the baseA tank leak means replacement, not a burner repair.

What to do before you call

Check that the gas valve to the heater is open and that the thermostat is set above the cold setting. Look for a tripped breaker if your heater has an electronic igniter. Do not take apart the gas control yourself. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911.
