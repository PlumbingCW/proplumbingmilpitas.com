When a failed water heater is an emergency

Replacement is urgent when the tank is leaking from the shell, when hot water is gone and cannot return or when the heater is unsafe.

Shut off the cold supply

The valve on the cold line above the tank stops new water from entering. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Switch off the fuel

Gas: turn the gas control to off. Electric: switch the heater's breaker off before anyone touches the tank.

Drain the puddle path

Move boxes and tools off the floor. A shop vacuum is useful for standing water in a garage.

Gather the label details

Photograph the data plate. Fuel, gallons and dimensions let us bring the right replacement.

What a code-compliant replacement includes in Milpitas

The City of Milpitas Office of Building Safety publishes a residential water heater handout. These are its main points.

  • Plumbing permitThe installation or replacement of a water heater requires a plumbing permit. A permit may be issued only to the building owner or the owner's authorized agent, and a contractor acting as agent must hold the proper State of California license classification.
  • Seismic strappingThe heater must be anchored or strapped to resist horizontal displacement, with straps in the upper and lower thirds of the tank.
  • Expansion tankRequired near the heater when the system has a pressure regulator or another normally closed device such as a check valve or backflow preventer.
  • Drain panWhere a leak could cause damage, a 1 1/2 inch deep watertight pan with a drain of at least 3/4 inch is required.
  • T&P discharge pipeIt must discharge by gravity through an air gap, 5 inches to 2 feet above the ground, pointing downward and not into the pan.
  • Gas connectionsGas heaters need a sediment trap downstream of the shutoff valve, and the city requires a seismic or excess-flow gas shutoff when the gas system is altered or added to.

The city says water heater replacement permits are available online. A final inspection follows the work.

The Bay Area zero-NOx rule: what it means if you replace now

The Bay Area Air Quality Management District adopts rules on appliance emissions. According to its building appliances page, the Air District board adopted amendments in 2026 that move the zero-NOx compliance date for residential water heaters to January 1, 2028, with exemptions for qualifying low-income owners and for properties with significant electrical or space constraints.

The district states the rule applies only when a water heater is replaced and does not require replacing a working unit. If you are replacing in 2026 or 2027, ask us what is permitted. If your replacement date is later, plan for a heat pump or other zero-NOx model, and tell us about your panel capacity before we quote.

Old garage heater, older house: what we often find

Many Milpitas homes from the 1960s through the 1980s keep the heater in a garage or a closet on an outside wall. Straps are often missing and the T&P pipe may point at the floor or end in a joint. We correct these during the swap.

If your tank is in the garage near a laundry area and the old one has been rusting at the bottom, ask us to look at the supply lines at the same time. A related water heater installation may be the right path if you are relocating the unit.
