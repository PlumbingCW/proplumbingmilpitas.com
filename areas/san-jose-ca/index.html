Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

Do these in order while the plumber is on the way.

Turn off the main valve

The shutoff is usually where the water line enters the house, often by the front hose bib or in the garage. Turn it clockwise to the end.

Cut power to wet rooms

If water is near outlets, a ceiling fixture or an appliance, switch that circuit off at the panel before you step in.

Clear the floor

Lift rugs, electronics and boxes out of the wet area. Photograph the damage for your insurer.

Call the 24/7 line

Say whether water is still running, which fixtures are affected and what street you are on.

Local note

Permits in San Jose are different from Milpitas

San Jose runs its own building permit system. The city says installing or replacing a water heater needs a plumbing permit, that single-family and duplex jobs can be applied for online at SJPermits.org and that the inspector is booked at SJPermits.org or on 408-535-3555. The permit card must stay on the property until the inspection is done.

The same city page warns that unpermitted work can cause insurance trouble, since insurers may deny claims related to it. For a repipe or a water service replacement, San Jose requires a plumbing permit, plus a building permit if shear walls, firewalls or structural members are affected. We pull the permit that matches the job and tell you when to expect the inspector. See repiping and water heater replacement.
