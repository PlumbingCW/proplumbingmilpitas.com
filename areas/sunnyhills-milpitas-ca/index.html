Plumber in Sunnyhills, Milpitas, CA

Sunnyhills is one of the older named neighborhoods in Milpitas, CA. It grew up in the mid-1950s beside the new Ford assembly plant, and the City of Milpitas still describes it as a historic neighborhood of mature single-family areas with small retail strip centers.

This page is for Sunnyhills homes and apartments. It covers who handles which pipe, what a plumber can and cannot tell from a build date, and when to call. To talk through a problem, call (510) 544-1962.

A neighborhood built alongside the Ford plant

Ford began building its Milpitas assembly plant in 1955. Sunnyhills was built nearby for the plant workers. KQED reports that it opened in 1956 on former ranch land, with the Ford union hall at its entrance. The United Auto Workers and Quaker-affiliated groups backed it as a planned integrated community, which is why local histories treat it as a landmark.

Gross Street, named for union housing leader and later Milpitas mayor Ben Gross, is in the neighborhood. We do not publish a boundary map here, because the City does not publish one that we could verify. If you are not sure we reach your street, give us your cross streets when you call and we confirm.

Plumbing questions that fit a mature neighborhood

These are general plumbing topics. They are not a claim that Sunnyhills homes share one particular pipe problem.

  • A drain that keeps backing upWhen the same drain stops more than once, the cause is usually in the pipe and not the fixture. See drain cleaning and sewer camera inspection.
  • A water heater that is leaking or coldTank heaters wear out and can fail at the base. See water heater repair.
  • Worn fixtures and shutoff valvesToilets, faucets and angle stops wear out in any house. See toilet repair and installation.
  • Wet spots or a high water billA meter that moves with every tap off means water is escaping somewhere. See leak detection.

The only way to know what is in your walls and under your yard is to look. That is why a camera inspection comes before a repair quote on a drain that repeats.

What we assume about homes of this age, and what we do not

We do not guess a pipe material from a build date. Many older homes have been remodeled or repiped since they were built, and two houses on one street can differ. Permit history from the City of Milpitas Office of Building Safety, the seller disclosures and a plumber looking at the pipe are better evidence than the year.

If you rent in the area, the City housing plan notes that Sunnyhills Apartments has 149 Section 8 units. Tenants should report leaks and clogs to the property manager first, because repairs in a rental normally need the owner to approve them.

Local note

Water in Milpitas is moderately hard

The City of Milpitas runs the water distribution system and buys water from the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission and Valley Water. Its 2024 water quality report gives an average hardness of 110.5 mg/L as calcium carbonate, with readings from 82 to 168 mg/L across the system. That is about 6.5 grains per gallon by our conversion.

Moderately hard water leaves scale on heating elements and fixtures over the years. If you see crust on a showerhead or hear a tank heater rumble, ask about water heater service or a softener or filter. The city disinfects with chloramine.

Source type: City of Milpitas 2024 Water Quality Report.
