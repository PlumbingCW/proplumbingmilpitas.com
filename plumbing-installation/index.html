Permits for plumbing work in Milpitas

The City of Milpitas Office of Building Safety requires a plumbing permit to install or replace water piping, drain piping, gas piping and fixtures in a building. Extensive removal of wall or ceiling structure also needs a building permit. Repairs that do not replace or rearrange pipe are exempt.

Permits normally mean two inspections: rough, before the walls close, and final, once the work is done. We schedule both and keep the walls open until the inspector has seen the work. A jobsite without a permit can cost more later, because an unpermitted remodel can trouble a home sale or an insurance claim.

Plan plumbing before the drywall and cabinets go in

The cheapest time to change plumbing is when walls are open. Moving a toilet or a sink after tile is down is costly. Decide fixture locations, shower valve type and whether you want a dedicated shutoff before work begins, and tell your contractor if you plan to include a tankless heater or a water softener.

If you are also replacing aging pipe, doing it during the remodel is the better time. See repiping.

Choosing materials and fixtures that last

Pipe material, valve quality and fixture finish decide how often you call a plumber again. Supply lines in a remodel are usually copper or PEX, both approved in Milpitas when they meet the California Plumbing Code and carry NSF/ANSI/CAN 61 certification for drinking water. Brass or quality quarter-turn stop valves beat cheap plastic ones that seize.

Fixtures are the part you see and use daily. Pick a faucet with a replaceable cartridge and a toilet with a standard flapper, so repairs stay simple in ten years. Tell us what you chose and we confirm that the rough-in, the drain size and the vent suit it before the walls close.

Keep the model numbers and manuals for anything we install. If a part fails later, the model number is the fastest way to the right replacement.
