Drains and sewer lines

Slow sinks and backed-up tubs start with drain cleaning. When grease or scale keeps coming back, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall. If the main line is the problem, sewer line cleaning clears it, and a sewer camera inspection shows what is inside. When the pipe itself is cracked or sagging, sewer line repair fixes it.

Leaks and broken pipes

A water meter that runs with taps off calls for leak detection. A leak under a concrete floor is covered under slab leak repair, and a pipe that has let go is a job for burst pipe repair. The pipe from the meter to your house is handled by water line repair and replacement, and a house with failing pipe throughout may need repiping.

Water heaters

No hot water or a leak at the tank starts with water heater repair. When the tank has reached the end of its life, water heater replacement covers the permit, strapping and gas safety work. Planned swaps and new locations fall under water heater installation, and on-demand units are covered on tankless water heater.

Fixtures, installs and water quality

Running and leaking toilets are covered under toilet repair and installation, and drips and weak flow under faucet repair. A jammed disposal is a job for garbage disposal repair and installation. Remodels and new fixtures are handled under plumbing installation, and scale and taste problems under water softener and filtration.

Businesses

Shops, offices, restaurants and warehouses are covered under commercial plumbing, with after-hours service for emergencies. If you are not sure which page fits your problem, call and describe it. We will point you to the right service and tell you what to expect.
