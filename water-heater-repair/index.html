When a water heater problem is an emergency

A heater that is only lukewarm can wait for a morning visit. A tank that is leaking, a unit that smells of gas or a T&P valve that is spraying cannot.

Gas smell

Leave the house, call 911, then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. Call us after you are safe.

Water on the floor

Turn the heater to off, then close the cold supply valve on the pipe above the tank. Move items out of the puddle.

Spraying valve

The temperature and pressure relief valve is a safety device. If it is discharging hot water, keep people clear and call.

Tripping breaker

An electric heater that keeps tripping needs its element or wiring checked. Leave the breaker off.

Repair or replace: the decision we walk through with you

A repair makes sense when the tank is sound and the failed part is cheap compared with a new unit. Replacement makes sense when the tank itself has leaked, when it is past roughly 10 to 12 years, or when parts and labor would approach the price of a new heater.

Age is on the data plate. The first four characters of the serial number usually encode the month and year the unit was made, and we read that on every visit. Tell us the age on the call and we will say whether a repair is likely worth it.

Gas, electric and hybrid units, repaired differently

  • Gas tank heatersPilot, thermocouple, gas control valve and venting. Venting problems get fixed before the heater is relit, because exhaust must leave the house.
  • Electric tank heatersUpper and lower heating elements, thermostats and wiring. We test with a meter before replacing a part.
  • Hybrid heat pump unitsFan, compressor, condensate drain and the backup element. Many of these installs are recent, so some repairs are warranty questions.
  • Tankless heatersScale on the heat exchanger and error codes. See tankless water heater for flush and descale service.

What Milpitas water does to a heater

The City of Milpitas 2024 water quality report lists average total hardness of about 57 ppm and 110 ppm for its two supplies, with individual readings between 8.4 and 168 ppm. In grains per gallon, that is roughly 3 to 6.5 on average. It is moderate, so sediment builds more slowly than in hard-water cities, but it still builds.

Flushing a tank annually helps. If your heater is more than a few years old and never flushed, a hard flush can dislodge a hardened layer and start a leak, so we test first.

Earthquake bracing and code points we check on every visit

The Hayward and Calaveras fault systems dominate the earthquake threat in central Santa Clara County, according to the city's earthquake page. Milpitas follows the California Plumbing Code, which requires water heaters to be anchored or strapped against horizontal displacement. The city's handout says straps go in the upper and lower thirds of the tank, with the lower strap at least 4 inches above the controls.

We also check for a T&P discharge pipe that runs downward without valves, an expansion tank where the system is closed and a drain pan where a leak could cause damage. Repairing a heater without fixing a missing strap leaves the house exposed in the next quake.

What to do first, and the code behind it

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us. Relief valve piping, strapping and gas shutoff expectations come from the City of Milpitas Office of Building Safety and the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).
