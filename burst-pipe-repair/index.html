When a pipe bursts: the first ten minutes

The order matters. Water first, then electricity, then belongings.

Close the main shutoff

Find the main valve at the house, often near the front hose bib, and turn it clockwise. If you cannot reach it, the city-side valve is near the meter.

Cut power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, ceiling fixtures or appliances, shut off those circuits at the panel before stepping into standing water.

Open the lowest faucet

This drains the line and lowers pressure. Flush a toilet once to empty the tank.

Call, then document

Call us, then photograph the damage and the broken pipe for any insurance claim.

Milpitas Public Works answers water emergencies on the city side of the meter at (408) 586-2600 during business hours and (408) 586-2400 after hours.

Why pipes burst in a mild climate like Milpitas

Hard freezes are rare in Milpitas, so most bursts come from other causes. High water pressure is a big one. The California Plumbing Code, in the city's handout, calls for a pressure regulator when pressure exceeds 80 psi, and a failed regulator can push pipes past their limit.

Corrosion, a failing joint and ground movement after an earthquake are the other common causes. A rare cold snap can freeze an exposed hose bib line or a pipe in an unheated attic or garage wall.

Pipes we repair

  • Copper supply linesPinholes from corrosion and failed solder joints in homes built from the 1960s through the 1980s.
  • PEX and CPVCFailed fittings, damaged runs and rodent chew marks. PEX in soil must be sleeved under the city handout.
  • Galvanized steelOld galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside and bursts at threads. A repipe is often the better outcome.
  • Drain and sewer linesA cracked drain can flood a wall cavity. For lateral breaks see sewer line repair.
  • Hose bibs and supply stopsSmall failures that run all night in a wall or garage.

After the pipe is fixed: drying and damage

We repair plumbing, not water damage restoration. After the pipe is fixed, wet drywall, insulation and flooring may need professional drying. Ask your insurer whether they have a preferred restoration company, and photograph everything before cleanup.

If you also suspect water in the walls, we can verify with leak detection before the walls are closed up.
