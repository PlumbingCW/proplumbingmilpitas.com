Signs you need an emergency plumber now

Some problems can wait for the next appointment and some cannot. These cannot.

Water spreading from a pipe

Close the main valve first. The longer it runs, the more the repair and the cleanup cost.

Sewage backing up

If waste comes up in a tub, shower or floor drain, stop using water and call.

No hot water in a cold week

A dead water heater with a leak at the base is an emergency. See water heater repair.

Water meter spinning with taps off

Hidden leaks waste water and can damage floors. See leak detection.

A smell of gas near the heater

Leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

Ceiling bulging or dripping

Water above you can collapse drywall. Shut off the main and call.

What to do while the plumber is on the way

The first ten minutes decide how much damage a leak does. Close the main shutoff, usually at the front of the house near the hose bib or in the garage. Open the lowest faucet to drain the line. If water is near outlets or a light fixture, turn off that circuit at the panel before you walk in.

Move rugs and electronics out of the way and take photos for your insurer. Then call us. Tell us whether water is still running, how many fixtures are affected and what your cross streets are. We use that to send the right plumber with the right parts. For a pipe that has burst, see burst pipe repair. For a backed-up line, see drain cleaning.

Water heater rules in Milpitas

The City of Milpitas requires a plumbing permit to replace a water heater. Tanks need two seismic straps, a thermal expansion tank where the system is closed, a temperature and pressure relief valve piped to a safe discharge and a seismic gas shutoff where required. We pull the permit and meet the inspector.

Regional rules are tightening for gas water heaters too. The Bay Area Air Quality Management District has adopted zero-NOx rules, and the date for new gas tanks has been pushed to 2028. We keep up with that, so the unit you buy today will not be a problem at inspection. See water heater replacement and tankless water heaters.
