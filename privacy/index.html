Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we receive when you contact Milpitas Pro Plumbing and how we use it. It is written in plain language and covers this website, proplumbingmilpitas.com.

What we collect

This website has no forms. We receive information only when you call or email us. That can include your name, phone number, address and a description of your plumbing problem. We use it to schedule and complete your service, to follow up on the work and to answer questions.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start and we will move to email.

How we use and share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with people who help us do the job, such as a permit office when a permit is needed, and where the law requires it. We keep records of work for as long as we need them for warranty, tax and legal reasons.

Our website may use basic technical logs that record the pages visited and the type of browser. These help us keep the site working and do not identify you.
